OUR WASH & DETAILING PROCESS

From the first inspection to the final walk-around, every car gets the same careful attention at Taqwa Automobile.

01

Pre-Wash Inspection

We check your car's condition and note any spots that need extra attention before we begin.

02

Exterior Hand Wash

A thorough hand wash with paint-safe shampoo, followed by a careful hand dry to avoid water spots.

03

Interior Vacuum & Cleaning

Seats, carpets, dashboard and door panels are vacuumed and wiped down to remove dust and grime.

04

Wax, Polish & Shine

Quality wax and polish restore gloss and add a protective layer against dust and sun damage.

05

Tire & Trim Detailing

Tires, rims and exterior trim are cleaned and dressed for a finished, showroom look.

06

Final Quality Check

We do a final walk-around with you to make sure every detail meets our standard before you drive off.
