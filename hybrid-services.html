BUILT FOR HYBRID TECHNOLOGY

Hybrid vehicles like the Toyota Prius, Aqua, Axio Hybrid and Corolla Hybrid run on complex battery, motor and inverter systems that a standard workshop isn't equipped to handle. At Taqwa Automobile, our technicians are specially trained on hybrid drivetrains, high-voltage battery packs and regenerative braking systems.

From battery diagnostics to full ECU scanning, we use manufacturer-approved tools and genuine parts to keep your hybrid running safely, efficiently and true to its factory performance.

High-Voltage Safety

Hybrid battery packs carry high-voltage current. Our technicians follow strict safety protocols to work on these systems without risk.

Complex Software Systems

Hybrid drivetrains rely on precise software calibration between the engine, motor and battery — errors here affect performance and safety.

Specialized Tools Required

Standard workshop equipment can't safely diagnose hybrid components. We use manufacturer-approved diagnostic tools built for the job.

Longer Battery Life

Correct maintenance and early diagnostics can significantly extend the life of your hybrid battery, saving you from costly early replacement.

OUR HYBRID SERVICE PROCESS

From first inspection to final road test, every hybrid that comes into our workshop follows the same careful, manufacturer-standard process.

01

Initial Hybrid Health Check

We start with a full visual and computer-based inspection covering the battery, inverter, motor and charging system.

02

Battery & High-Voltage Diagnostics

Cell-by-cell battery testing and voltage checks to pinpoint exactly which cells or components need attention.

03

Certified Repair or Replacement

Faulty parts are repaired or replaced using genuine, manufacturer-approved components — never generic substitutes.

04

System Calibration & ECU Reset

After any repair, we recalibrate the hybrid control software so every system works together as the manufacturer intended.

05

Full Road Test

Every hybrid is road tested under real driving conditions to confirm smooth power delivery and regenerative braking performance.

06

Warranty & Ongoing Support

We back our hybrid work with a service warranty and are always available for follow-up checks and questions.
