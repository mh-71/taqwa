WHAT IS LPG CONVERSION?

LPG (Liquefied Petroleum Gas) conversion is the process of fitting your petrol or diesel vehicle with an additional fuel system that allows it to run on LPG. The engine keeps its original fuel option intact, so you can switch between petrol and gas at the push of a button.

At Taqwa Automobile, every conversion is carried out by experienced technicians using genuine, government-approved kits. From initial inspection to final road test, we make sure your vehicle runs safely, efficiently, and smoothly on gas.

OUR CONVERSION PROCESS

A clear, step-by-step process from the moment you arrive to the moment you drive off on gas.

01

Vehicle Inspection & Consultation

We inspect your engine and discuss the right kit and cylinder size for your vehicle and driving needs.

02

Kit & Cylinder Selection

You choose from certified, government-approved LPG kits and cylinders that suit your budget and vehicle type.

03

Professional Installation

Our trained technicians fit the reducer, injectors, cylinder, and wiring with precision and care.

04

ECU Calibration & Tuning

The system is calibrated so your engine runs smoothly and efficiently on both petrol and gas.

05

Safety & Leak Testing

Every connection and valve is pressure-tested to confirm there are no leaks before you drive away.

06

Road Test & Handover

We take your car for a final road test and walk you through the system before handover, backed by our 2-year warranty.
